Privacy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

Unique for Logistics LLC (“we”) publishes this site to describe Cycle CRM, a sales system for a motor carrier’s desk, and to receive requests for a first meeting.

This website

The meeting form does not store a message on this site. It opens an email to info@cycletiger.com on your device. If you send that email, we use your name, company, email, and note to answer you and to prepare the meeting.

We do not sell personal information. We do not run advertising trackers on this page.

The Cycle system

When a carrier uses Cycle CRM, the sales book stays in that carrier’s system. That data can include companies, people, MC numbers, leads, opportunities, call and email history, the next step, sources, and which salesperson owns the company.

An MC number can be used to read a public FMCSA record onto the company: address, phone, and license status.

Cycle CRM can be put in with Cycle TMS, with another transportation system, or on its own. A carrier agreement covers retention, who may see a company, and how a carrier can ask for export or deletion of its data. This page is not that agreement.
